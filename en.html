FAQ

Quick
answers.

Do I need a visa for Canada?

It depends on your nationality and travel history. Many travellers only need an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA); others need a visitor visa. Next guides you through the whole process well in advance.

Do I need to speak English?

It helps, but it isn’t required. The Executive Mission includes a bilingual team sized to your meeting agenda.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier, the better. Hotels near the Convention Centre sell out early, and the most sought-after meetings are booked months ahead.

What is Toronto like in March?

It’s still winter, with temperatures around freezing. The whole show is indoors, and in your free time you can visit the CN Tower and Niagara Falls, less than 2 hours from the city.
